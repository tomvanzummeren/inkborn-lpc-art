# Inkborn's edits of Liberated Pixel Cup art

These sheets are Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) art, re-gridded and recoloured for the game Inkborn, shared under CC-BY-SA 4.0 (LICENSE.txt) at https://github.com/tomvanzummeren/inkborn-lpc-art. They are made from these LPC parts and creatures, each by the authors named, used under the licence named (of the ones each offers).
